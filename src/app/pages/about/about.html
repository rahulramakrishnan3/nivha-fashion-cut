Our Journey

Stitching Trust, One Outfit At A Time

Nivha By Fashion Cut began as a humble tailoring shop dedicated to the women of Koppam and the wider Palakkad community. What started with simple alterations and blouse stitching has grown into a full-service ladies tailoring and embroidery boutique — trusted for everything from everyday wear to bridal outfits.

Located conveniently near Sree Krishna Sweets at Koppam Junction, our team combines traditional stitching expertise with an eye for modern design, ensuring every customer walks out with an outfit that fits perfectly and feels uniquely theirs.

Our Mission
